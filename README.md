# Mama Meadow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Mama Meadow ("the app") is made by Nirvana D'Souza. This policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

## Information you enter
You may enter information such as journal entries, baby details, moods, and notes. This is used only to provide the app's features to you. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## AI companion
When you chat with the AI companion, your messages are sent to an AI service only to create a reply. The AI companion is not a doctor and does not give medical advice. Please contact a healthcare professional for medical concerns.

## Payments and subscriptions
All purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see or store your card or payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

## Children
The app is meant for adults (parents and caregivers). It is not directed at children.

## Your choices
You can delete your information by deleting it in the app or by deleting the app. You can also email us to ask for your data to be removed.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page.
