MAMA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

MAMA is a motherhood companion designed to help mothers care for, track, and preserve memories of their children.

Information We Collect

MAMA may collect information that users choose to provide, including mother profile information, child or baby profile information, journal entries, memories, feeding records, sleep records, diaper records, and other information entered by the user.

How We Use Information

Information provided by users is used to provide, personalize, maintain, and improve the features of the MAMA application.

Children's Information

MAMA is intended for parents and caregivers. Users should only enter information about a child when they are authorized to do so. MAMA does not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information provided through the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate and maintain the application.

Data Deletion

Users may contact us to request deletion of their personal information and associated data, subject to any legal or operational requirements.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to provide hosting, analytics, authentication, storage, or other technical functionality. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be made available through this webpage.
